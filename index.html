Cervical Spine & Upper Neck

The cervical vertebrae support the entire weight of your head and enable high degrees of motion. Subtle misalignments here often compress cranial nerves and trigger tension patterns radiating up to the head or down into the shoulders.

Frequent Symptoms:
Tension Headaches & Migraines Neck Stiffness / "Tech Neck" Tingling in Arms or Fingers Jaw (TMJ) Discomfort
Targeted Protocol:
Gentle Blair/Toggle adjustment, cervical decompression & trigger point therapy.
