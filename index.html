Clinical Care

Specialized Athletic Treatments

Select a category below to see how our treatments support speed, power, rotational torque, and joint longevity.

Spine and Joint

High Velocity Adjustments

Precise joint cavitation targeting restricted vertebrae, sacroiliac joints, and extremities to restore natural articular motion and reduce neural inhibition.

Focus: Thoracic extension, hip capsule clearance, rib dysfunction.
Soft Tissue

Active Release and Myofascial Mobilization

Hands-on tension release applied while the athlete moves through an active range of motion. Breaks down dense scar tissue and restores muscle glide.

Focus: Rotator cuff impingement, hamstring pulls, tight hip flexors.
Soft Tissue

Instrument Assisted Soft Tissue (IASTM)

Beveled stainless steel tools designed to locate and remediate fascial adhesions, stimulate localized blood flow, and initiate accelerated tissue remodeling.

Focus: Shin splints, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, Achilles tendinopathy.
Active Movement

Dynamic Neuromuscular Stabilization

Functional stabilization protocols based on developmental kinesiology to activate deep spinal stabilizers, improve intra-abdominal pressure, and build core control.

Focus: Squat depth, throwing mechanics, rotational core stability.
Spine and Joint

Extremity Joint Mechanics

Sports chiropractic extends beyond the back. We restore alignment and gliding mechanics in ankles, knees, wrists, and shoulders to optimize force transfer.

Focus: Ankle dorsiflexion, patellar tracking, AC joint restrictions.
Active Movement

Return-to-Play Movement Screen

Video assessment and load testing to pinpoint asymmetric compensation patterns before an athlete resumes full contact or maximal velocity training.

Focus: Post-injury clearance, lateral shift mitigation, landing mechanics.
Target Audience Focus

Customized for your stage of competition

A 14-year-old soccer player has different musculoskeletal demands than a 26-year-old powerlifter. We calibrate our treatment according to age and sport demands.

Ages 12 to 17

Scholastic and Club Athletes

Growing bodies frequently battle Osgood-Schlatter disease, Sever disease, posture strain from heavy school bags, and repetitive sport specialization. Our care is gentle, conservative, and emphasizes foundational core control.

Ages 18 to 23

Collegiate and Varsity Level

High volume training and rigorous schedules leave little time for lingering injuries. We provide swift acute pain mitigation, joint release, and movement maintenance to prevent missed competitions and preserve scholarships.

Ages 24 to 30

Post-Grad and Competitive Adults

Balancing career workloads with high intensity training like CrossFit, marathons, or recreational leagues. We address accumulated microtrauma, hip mobility limits, and disc preservation to keep your athletic peak intact.
